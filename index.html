Articles are published directly on your primary website domain so they can contribute to the search visibility and authority of the website you own.

Does the Blog Need to Appear in the Main Navigation?

No.

The article library can be connected through the footer, relevant service pages, internal links, resource categories, and search results without adding a prominent Blog item to the main navigation.

No company can guarantee a specific ranking, traffic increase, backlink result, lead volume, or placement within an AI-generated answer.

Results depend on the website, competition, content quality, technical health, existing authority, backlink profile, and market demand.
